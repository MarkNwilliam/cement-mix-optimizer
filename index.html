Clinker phases are derived from oxide chemistry; the optimizer substitutes low-cost additions up to their caps while keeping C3S, C3A, SO₃ and LOI inside the targets above.
Typical rule of thumb — each 1% of clinker replaced by a cheap addition saves roughly the cost difference per tonne, but must not force SO₃ over the setting-control window or pull fineness too low. This tool makes that trade-off explicit.
